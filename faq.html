Taxes

No. This is bookkeeping, not a CPA firm, and we don't file returns or give tax advice. Monthly books stay CPA-ready. An optional year-end package ($1,000–$3,000) covers the December close, a full-year review, 1099s for your subs, and a CPA-ready package.

We won't promise a number, and be skeptical of anyone who does. What we can say is that deductions you can't substantiate are deductions your CPA can't take — so documenting them as they happen, rather than reconstructing them in March, tends to change the outcome. The mechanism is documentation, not a loophole.

Some firms include a running estimate of what to hold back for taxes. We don't build tax planning into the monthly plan, and we don't give tax advice. Your CPA sets the actual number. If you want a year-end package with 1099s and a CPA-ready close, that's an optional $1,000–$3,000 add-on.
