One-time and optional

The costs that aren't your monthly plan.

Books Checkup — $250

A written review of every account plus a flat quote, paid before the review. Credited in full on your first invoice if you hire us within 30 days: against catch-up, or against the onboarding fee when your books are already current.

Catch-up / cleanup — from $500

One flat price for every month you're behind, quoted before work starts and paid upfront. The quote is the higher of your monthly price × months behind × 1.0, 1.25, or 1.5, or $4 × the transactions in those months, with a $500 minimum.

Onboarding — one month's fee

Only when your books are already current. Equal to one month of your plan, billed with the first month ahead. Waived for a brand-new business with no history.

Year-end package — $1,000–$3,000, optional

December close, a full-year review, 1099s for your subs, and a CPA-ready package. Not a tax return, and not included in the monthly plan. Add-ons of $50–$100/mo are available for extra accounts, vendor bill entry, invoice entry and A/R review, or W-9 tracking.

The guarantee

Books done by the 5th, or that month is free.

If your books aren't done by 11:59 PM Central on the 5th, that month's monthly fee is credited on your next invoice. It's written into the engagement letter. We guarantee delivery because that's the part we control — we won't promise tax savings or profit outcomes, and you should be wary of firms that do.

Fine print. "Done" means every bank and card account categorized and reconciled for the prior month, and your P&L, P&L by Job, and Balance Sheet emailed, by 11:59 PM Central on the 5th. The guarantee applies when, by the 2nd, your bank feeds are connected (or statements for unconnected accounts are sent) and every question we sent before month-end is answered. Questions about the last few days of the month can stay flagged in the report without breaking the guarantee. It doesn't apply to the first month of service, catch-up or year-end work, or a month when your invoice is unpaid.
