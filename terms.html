4. The on-time guarantee

Our published guarantee is that books are done by the 5th of every month. If not, that month is free: the monthly fee is credited on your next invoice. "Done" means every bank and card account categorized and reconciled for the prior month, and your P&L, P&L by Job, and Balance Sheet emailed, by 11:59 PM Central on the 5th. The guarantee applies when, by the 2nd, your bank feeds are connected (or statements for unconnected accounts are sent) and every question we sent before month-end is answered. Questions about the last few days of the month can stay flagged in the report without breaking the guarantee. If we miss, that month's monthly fee is credited on your next invoice. It doesn't apply to the first month of service, catch-up or year-end work, or a month when your invoice is unpaid. This guarantee applies to delivery timing only and is not a guarantee of any financial, tax or business outcome. The engagement letter controls if there is a conflict.
