Start with the honest version

Bookkeeping isn't the thing you're buying.

You're buying the answer to one question: was that deal, or that job, worth doing?

Nobody wakes up wanting transactions categorized. They want to stop guessing. The reason ordinary bookkeeping feels like an expense rather than an investment is that it produces a tidy record of the past and stops there — technically correct, strategically useless. You get a P&L that tells you the business made money in aggregate, which is exactly the level of detail at which you can't do anything about it.

For an agent, aggregate hides the fact that referral-sourced deals net you nearly double what portal leads do after cost. For a contractor, aggregate hides the fact that one category of work has been running at a loss all year while the rest carries it. The number you need isn't monthly. It's per unit of work.

Why ordinary bookkeeping isn't enough

Three gaps that a generalist can't close for you.

No cost allocation

A generic chart of accounts records that you spent $4,100 on materials. It cannot tell you which job consumed it, so job margin is unknowable no matter how clean the books are.

Reports that arrive too late

A P&L that shows up in the third week of the month is history you can't act on. Monthly clients get books done by the 5th, or that month is free.

Books that fell behind the work

Catch-up is a one-time, upfront quote from $500 — not an open-ended hourly mess — so you know the cost before work starts.

Our mechanism

Job-level books, closed on time every month.

The same work on every plan. Your tier is just the volume of transactions, about $4 each, rounded up to Starter, Standard, or Plus.

A chart of accounts designed around your jobs

For contractors, that means job phases, change orders, retainage and sub payments structured so costs attach to the job that caused them. It is set up once, during catch-up or onboarding, and it is what makes a P&L by Job useful instead of decorative.

Weekly categorization, reviewed by a person

Every bank and card transaction is categorized weekly and tagged to the right job or class. AI tools help with volume; a ProAdvisor reviews the close. Shared costs get allocated to the job that caused them, so margin by job is a number you can use on the next bid.

Reports by the 5th, or that month is free

You get a P&L, P&L by Job, and Balance Sheet, plus a short notes email, by 11:59 PM Central on the 5th. If we miss it, that month's fee is credited on your next invoice. Forecasting, payroll, and tax returns are not included.

Risk reversal

Books done by the 5th, or that month is free.

If your books aren't done by 11:59 PM Central on the 5th, that month's monthly fee is credited on your next invoice. It's in the engagement letter, so you can hold us to it without arguing about a marketing page.

Fine print. "Done" means every bank and card account categorized and reconciled for the prior month, and your P&L, P&L by Job, and Balance Sheet emailed, by 11:59 PM Central on the 5th. The guarantee applies when, by the 2nd, your bank feeds are connected (or statements for unconnected accounts are sent) and every question we sent before month-end is answered. Questions about the last few days of the month can stay flagged in the report without breaking the guarantee. It doesn't apply to the first month of service, catch-up or year-end work, or a month when your invoice is unpaid.

We guarantee delivery because delivery is what we control. We will never promise a tax saving, an audit outcome, or a profit figure — those depend on your business and your CPA, and any firm promising otherwise is selling you something they can't deliver.

You also aren't locked in. Month to month, billed a month ahead. Cancel with 30 days' notice and your QuickBooks file, your chart of accounts and your templates go with you.

Fit

Who this is for — and who it isn't.

We turn down work that isn't a fit, because a bad fit costs you money and costs us a reference.

This is for you if

  • You're a trade contractor — concrete, paving, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodelers — or a similar job-based business
  • You can't currently say what a specific job netted you
  • The books are behind, or reports arrive too late in the month to use
  • You want job-level books, closed by the 5th, at a flat monthly price
  • You're willing to keep bank feeds connected and answer questions by the 2nd

This isn't for you if

  • You need tax returns filed or tax advice; we're not a CPA firm
  • You want payroll, sales tax filings, bill pay, or cash-flow forecasts bundled into the monthly fee
  • You need someone to pay bills or move money
  • You're pre-revenue and don't have transactions to close yet — start when the books exist
